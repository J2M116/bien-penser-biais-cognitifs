# Authority Bias

## Short

We give too much weight to a claim because it comes from a figure of authority or prestige.

## Example

An unsourced figure seems reliable because it is quoted by a famous professor speaking outside their field of expertise.

## Detailed description

Status can be used as a cue to competence when we lack time or knowledge. The bias arises when status replaces an examination of relevant expertise, method, and evidence.

## Why it matters

Authority strongly influences health, work, politics, media, and the use of expert or AI systems.

## Limits and nuances

The label covers several mechanisms involving obedience, prestige, and credibility; it should not be treated as an exact synonym for the halo effect.

## Prevention

- Check the source's specific expertise and conflicts of interest.
- When possible, assess the argument without knowing its author's name.
