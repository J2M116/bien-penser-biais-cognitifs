# Automation Bias

## Short

We follow an automated system's recommendation too readily, even when it contradicts reliable cues.

## Example

An analyst approves an alert generated by an AI despite a visible inconsistency in the source data.

## Detailed description

Automation can cause commission errors, when a person follows a bad recommendation, and omission errors, when they fail to detect what the system did not flag. Trust, workload, and the way the recommendation is presented all shape the effect.

## Why it matters

As algorithmic and AI tools become widespread, its practical scope is very broad and sometimes critical.

## Limits and nuances

An accurate automated recommendation often improves a decision; the problem is miscalibrated trust, not automation itself.

## Prevention

- Require the system to display the data and uncertainties supporting its recommendation.
- Include cases in which the user must form a judgment before seeing the system's output.
