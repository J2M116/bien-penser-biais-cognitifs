# Availability Cascade

## Short

An idea seems increasingly plausible because it is repeated publicly and becomes easy to bring to mind.

## Example

A rumor frequently repeated in the media eventually seems established, even though every report traces back to the same source.

## Detailed description

Social repetition increases the mental availability of a belief; the reactions it prompts then produce further repetition. This loop can create the appearance of consensus or independent evidence.

## Why it matters

It helps explain how risks, rumors, and moral panics are amplified in media environments.

## Limits and nuances

The concept describes a collective process as much as an individual bias; its importance depends heavily on the information ecosystem.

## Prevention

- Trace a claim back to its original source rather than counting repetitions.
- Distinguish the number of publications, the number of independent sources, and the quality of the evidence.
