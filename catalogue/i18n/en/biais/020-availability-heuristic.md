# Availability Heuristic

## Short

We judge events that come easily to mind as more likely or frequent.

## Example

After seeing several reports about plane crashes, a person overestimates their frequency relative to road accidents.

## Detailed description

Ease of recall depends on recency, emotion, media coverage, and personal experience. It can sometimes provide a useful cue, but it cannot replace a representative base rate.

## Why it matters

It is involved in nearly every intuitive estimate of frequency or risk.

## Limits and nuances

It is not exactly the same as an availability cascade, which adds a social dynamic of amplification.

## Prevention

- Look for frequency data from a representative sample.
- Ask which examples would come to mind if you had been exposed to different information.
