# Barnum Effect

## Short

We judge vague and very general descriptions to be personally accurate.

## Example

A horoscope saying that you enjoy being with others but sometimes need solitude seems to describe your personality precisely.

## Detailed description

Positive, flexible wording that applies to many people encourages personal identification. Readers mentally fill in ambiguities with their own memories.

## Why it matters

It is particularly relevant to unvalidated personality tests, astrology, mentalism, and automatically personalized messages.

## Limits and nuances

Sensitivity varies with the wording, the credibility attributed to the source, and prior belief in the procedure.

## Prevention

- Ask what proportion of people would accept the same description.
- Blind-test the description against several possible profiles.
