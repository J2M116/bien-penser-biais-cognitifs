# Compassion Fade

## Short

Our emotional response does not increase in proportion to the number of people in distress and may even diminish.

## Example

The story of one identifiable victim elicits more donations than an abstract presentation of thousands of victims.

## Detailed description

Emotion is more readily elicited by an identifiable individual than by a statistic or a group. As the scale increases, it can produce emotional numbing, a sense of helplessness, or a weaker affective representation.

## Why it matters

It influences charitable giving, humanitarian policy, public health, and media coverage of disasters.

## Limits and nuances

Results depend on how individuals and the group are presented; the effect is not a fixed moral incapacity.

## Prevention

- Pair statistics with representative stories without substituting those stories for the data.
- Present the concrete action that can be taken and the marginal impact of providing help.
