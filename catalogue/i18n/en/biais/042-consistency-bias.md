# Consistency Bias

## Short

We reconstruct our past attitudes or behavior as being closer to our present state than they actually were.

## Example

After changing their opinion of a political party, a person claims they always had reservations that they had not expressed before.

## Detailed description

Autobiographical memory is not an exact record; it is reconstructed from our current identity and beliefs. This reconstruction creates a subjective continuity that can exceed the actual continuity.

## Why it matters

It reduces the quality of retrospective testimony and assessments of personal or organizational change.

## Limits and nuances

The term is used in several ways and can overlap with hindsight bias; this entry should remain focused on reconstructing the past from the standpoint of the present.

## Prevention

- Keep dated records of opinions, forecasts, and decisions.
- Compare memories with contemporary documents before interpreting a change over time.
