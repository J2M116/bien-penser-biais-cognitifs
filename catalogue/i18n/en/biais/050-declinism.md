# Declinism

## Short

We tend to idealize the past and perceive the present or future as part of a general decline.

## Example

Each generation claims that young people are less respectful and that society used to be safer, despite more nuanced data.

## Detailed description

Disproportionate exposure to present-day bad news and the faster fading of some negative memories can create an asymmetrical comparison. Decline can of course be real in a specific area; the bias lies in concluding that it exists without comparable indicators.

## Why it matters

It influences social diagnoses, political nostalgia, resource allocation, and perceptions of risk.

## Limits and nuances

The concept is broad and was long poorly standardized; more recent research focuses specifically on the illusion of moral decline.

## Prevention

- Compare indicators defined in the same way across several periods.
- Distinguish change in a specific area from a global judgment about society.
