# Dunning-Kruger Effect

## Short

Low competence can be accompanied by a limited ability to recognize one's own mistakes.

## Example

A beginner overestimates the quality of their analysis because they have not yet mastered the criteria needed to see its flaws.

## Detailed description

The skills needed to perform a task successfully can also help someone evaluate their performance. However, the popular claim that the least competent people are always the most confident oversimplifies the findings and may be amplified by statistical effects.

## Why it matters

Confidence calibration matters in learning, recruitment, expertise, and public communication.

## Limits and nuances

The interpretation, magnitude, and some of the methods used to demonstrate the effect are debated; this entry should not be used to label or ridicule others.

## Prevention

- Ask for numerical predictions and provide repeated objective feedback.
- Evaluate work using external criteria and benchmark examples.
