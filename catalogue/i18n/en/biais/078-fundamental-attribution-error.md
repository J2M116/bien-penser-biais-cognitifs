# Fundamental Attribution Error

## Short

We explain other people's behavior too much in terms of their personality and not enough in terms of their situation.

## Example

Someone's lateness is attributed to carelessness without considering disrupted transportation or contradictory instructions.

## Detailed description

An observer can easily see the person acting but has less visibility into the constraints surrounding them. The observer may therefore overestimate stable dispositions and underestimate roles, pressures, and opportunities.

## Why it matters

It influences management, justice, relationships, performance evaluation, and the interpretation of political behavior.

## Limits and nuances

The strength of the phenomenon depends on culture, the information available, and point of view; the word “fundamental” does not mean universal.

## Prevention

- List at least three situational explanations before drawing a conclusion about personality.
- Compare the behavior of several people placed in the same situation.
