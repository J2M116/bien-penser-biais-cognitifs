# Hostile Attribution Bias

## Short

We interpret an ambiguous action as deliberately hostile.

## Example

A brief, awkwardly worded message is read as a personal attack rather than as a response written in a hurry.

## Detailed description

When intent is uncertain, expectations of threat can steer interpretation toward hostility. That interpretation can then provoke an aggressive response that appears to confirm the initial expectation.

## Why it matters

It plays a role in interpersonal conflict, bullying, polarization, and the moderation of ambiguous content.

## Limits and nuances

Much of the literature concerns aggression and certain populations; an individual should not be diagnosed on the basis of one isolated example.

## Prevention

- Generate several possible intentions before responding.
- Ask for factual clarification when doing so would not carry too high a relational cost.
