# Ostrich Effect

## Short

We avoid useful information when it is likely to be unpleasant or threatening.

## Example

An investor checks a portfolio less often during a downturn and consequently delays a necessary decision.

## Detailed description

Avoidance reduces discomfort in the short term but can increase future costs. It concerns the acquisition of information, which distinguishes it from simply rejecting information after reading it.

## Why it matters

It is important in health, finance, safety, project management, and performance monitoring.

## Limits and nuances

Not consulting information can be rational when it is costly or cannot inform action; the avoidance must be shown to be motivated by the information's negative valence.

## Prevention

- Schedule mandatory times in advance for reviewing key indicators.
- Link negative information to a specific, feasible action.
