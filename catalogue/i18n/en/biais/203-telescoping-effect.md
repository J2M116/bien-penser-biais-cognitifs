# Telescoping Effect

## Short

We remember recent events as older, or distant events as more recent, than they actually are.

## Example

Someone says that an incident happened last month when it actually occurred three months ago.

## Detailed description

The temporal location of a memory is reconstructed from imperfect cues. Forward telescoping brings older events closer to the present, while backward telescoping pushes some recent events farther into the past.

## Why it matters

It matters in retrospective surveys, witness accounts, medical histories, and measurements of past behavior.

## Limits and nuances

The effect mainly concerns dating and depends on the recall period, salience, and the reference points available.

## Prevention

- Use calendar landmarks and dated records.
- Ask for a date range and degree of confidence rather than forcing an exact date.
